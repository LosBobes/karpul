# Karpul user guide

Karpul is where colleagues share rides: who drives where and when, who sits in which car, and
who has the shared (company) cars. This guide starts with a short tutorial for your first
day, then explains every part of the app. The last section is for admins.

The app speaks English and Serbian; the names of buttons below are the English ones.

The short version of this guide is in the app itself: ☰ → **Help** → **How to use Karpul**.

## 1. Getting started

### Create your account

Open Karpul in your browser and choose **Register**. You need:

- a **username** (lowercase letters, digits, dot, dash or underscore),
- your **first name** and **surname**: this is the name colleagues see in a car,
- a **password** of at least 8 characters,
- optionally an **email** (so you can sign in with it instead of the username) and a
  **mobile phone** number. The phone number is shown on the rides you drive, so your
  passengers can call you. Leave it out if you never drive.

If a colleague already put you in their car by name before you had an account, registering
under that same name makes those seats yours automatically.

You stay signed in. As long as you open Karpul at least once a month, you never see the
sign-in screen again on that browser.

**Forgot your password?** There is no email reset. Ask an admin for a reset link; it works
once, for 48 hours, and signs you out everywhere else.

### Install it on your phone (recommended)

Karpul is a web app you can install like any other app. It then opens full screen from your
home screen, starts instantly and can send you notifications.

- **Android / Chrome / Edge:** the board shows an *Install Karpul* card. Tap **Install**.
- **iPhone / iPad:** tap **Share** in Safari's bar, then **Add to Home Screen**. On an
  iPhone this is required for notifications.
- Put the card away with **Not now**; you can bring it back later from
  ☰ → **Settings** → **Install the app**.

### Language and theme

☰ → **Settings** holds the **language** switch (English / Serbian) and the **theme**
(light, dark or follow the phone). The language switch is also on the sign-in screen.

## 2. Finding your way around

**The top bar** has the ☰ menu button, the title and a small lamp. A lit lamp means the board
is live: what colleagues do shows up on your screen as it happens, with no need to refresh.

**The three views.** On a phone they are the tabs at the bottom; on a computer, the switch in
the toolbar. Karpul remembers the one you used last.

| View | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Upcoming** | Every ride from today for the next 90 days, one heading per day, one row per car. The quickest way to find a seat. |
| **Week** | One week at a time. Pick a day on the strip at the top, then swipe through that day's cars. |
| **Day** | One day as a schedule of the shared cars (and own cars on the road), as a timeline or a clock. The best view to see when a shared car is free and book it. |

**The green plus** at the bottom right (on a computer, the **+** at the right of the toolbar)
adds a ride. It is the only add button in the app.

**The ☰ menu** holds everything that is not about one ride:

- your name at the top: **Your account**,
- **Your rides**, **Car status** and the **Shared car guide**,
- **Manage** (admins only): approvals, shared cars, people, all rides, the admin log,
- **Help**: the guide, **Contact support**, **Report a bug or send feedback**,
- **Settings** at the foot: **Notifications**, **Install the app**, theme and language.

On a computer the menu is always open down the left side.

The phone's **back button** closes whatever sheet or menu is on top, so it never throws you
out of the app.

## 3. Tutorial: get a seat in a car

1. Open **Upcoming**. Use the chips at the top to narrow the list: **Free seats** hides the
   full cars. Type a place or a driver's name in the search box to find a ride going your way.
2. **Tap a row.** The ride opens: the driver, when it **Leaves** and **Returns**, the route
   from start to destination with any pickup points, and who is already in the car.
3. If the ride has pickup points, choose where you get in with **Get in at**. If none of them
   suits you, choose **Somewhere else…**, type the place and where on the way it is; it is
   added to the route and the driver is told.
4. Tap **Get in this car**. Your name appears in the passenger list and the driver gets a
   notification.

That is it. Some more things you can do from there:

- **Leave:** tap **Leave** next to your name. The driver is told.
- **Switch cars:** if you are already in a car that day, the button on another car reads
  **Switch to this car**. One tap moves you.
- **Drag and drop:** in the **Week** view you can also drag your chip from the **You** tray
  onto a car, from one car to another, or back to **You** to get out. On a phone, press and
  hold your chip for a moment until it lifts, then drag.
- **Change your pickup point** later with **Change pickup**.
- **Call the driver:** if the driver gave a phone number, **Call the driver** is on the card.
- **Write to the car:** open **Messages** on the ride. Everyone in the car is notified.
  Use it for "running five minutes late", "I have a big suitcase", and so on.

**The car is full?** Tap **Wait for a seat**. The first seat that frees up is given to you
automatically and you get a notification. Getting a seat in any other car that day takes you
off the waiting lists for that day. **Stop waiting** takes you off by hand.

## 4. Tutorial: offer a ride

1. Tap the green **+**.
2. The form opens on **Simple**, which asks only what every ride needs:
   - **Car:** **Shared car** (pick which one) or **My own car** (type a short description,
     e.g. "grey Octavia", so passengers can find it).
   - **Passenger seats:** how many people you can take. For a shared car you cannot offer
     more seats than the car has.
   - **When:** the day, the departure time and the return time. Turn on **One way** if there
     is no way back. Rides are same-day.
   - **Route:** **From** and **To**.
3. Tap **Add ride**. It appears on everyone's board at once.

**Going and coming back on different days?** With **One way** on, a new ride offers **Add the
way back too**: give the day and time back and Karpul adds a second one-way ride with the
route reversed.

**Advanced** (the switch at the top of the form) shows the rest, in place:

- **Repeat every week** until a date, up to 26 weeks ahead. One ride is created per week,
  same weekday; if a shared car is taken on one of those weeks, nothing is created and the
  message names the date.
- **Extra pickup points:** up to 6 places, before the start or on the way, where passengers
  can get in.
- **Passengers can add and remove each other:** lets anyone already in the car put a
  colleague in or take one out (useful for a team trip). You can always do both.
- **Passengers can add pickup points:** on by default. Turn it off if your route is fixed;
  passengers then choose only from the points already on it.
- **Distance** (one way, in km) and **Chip in** (per seat, e.g. "300 din"). Nothing is
  charged in the app; it is only the agreement, written where passengers see it. Between the
  Beograd and Novi Sad offices the distance fills itself in.
- **Coffee:** say you would appreciate a coffee. It is only shown on the card.
- **Notes:** meeting point, detours, luggage.

Switching back to **Simple** keeps what you filled in; the foot of the form lists the extras
that are still set.

### Running your ride

Open your ride and use the **⋮** menu:

- **Edit ride**, **Duplicate ride**, **Remove ride**. On a weekly ride there is also
  **Remove this and following rides**.
- **Let passengers manage seats** / **Let passengers add pickup points** (and their "Stop…"
  opposites).
- **Share ride** and **Add to calendar** (see [section 8](#8-notifications-calendar-and-sharing)).

On the card itself you can **Add a passenger by name** (a colleague without an account works
too), take a passenger out, and **Add a pickup point** under the route.

When you change the date, the times or the route, or remove the ride, your passengers are
notified. Passengers of a removed ride are also told how many other cars that day still have
a free seat.

## 5. Everything about a ride

What you see on a ride card:

- **Driver** with their photo, and the car: *Shared car* or *Own car*, and whether it is
  electric, hybrid, diesel or petrol.
- **Leaves / Returns** (or *One way*), **From / To** and the pickup points in route order.
- Tags such as **Weekly** (part of a weekly series), **Coffee**, **Open list** (passengers
  manage the list), **Awaiting approval** and **Needs a driver** (both explained in
  [section 6](#6-shared-cars)).
- **Passengers in this car**, with where each gets in.
- **Waiting for a seat**, when someone is queued.
- **Messages**, the ride's thread. The author or the driver can remove a message.

**Who may change the passenger list:** the driver (and admins) always; anybody may always
add or remove *themselves*; other passengers only when the driver turned on *Passengers can
add and remove each other*.

**"No account" and "This is me".** Some older rides (for example the shared car's paper log
that was brought into the app) have a driver who is just a name with no account; they show a
*No account* tag. If that name is yours, you see **This is me**: tap it and all such rides under
that name become yours.

**Day view tips.** Tap a ride's bar to open it beside or below the plan. Tap a free stretch of
a shared car to start a new ride in that car at that time (it fills in the car, the start
and the time it is free until). Use **Show the day as** to switch between **Clock** and
**Timeline**. On a phone the strip on top (**The whole day**) lets you jump around the day.

## 6. Shared cars

Shared cars are the company's pool cars. Anyone can book one by adding a ride in it; Karpul
makes sure the same car is never booked twice at the same time. A car is booked from the
departure to the return time; a ride with no return time keeps the car until midnight.

### The fair share

To keep the pool fair, each person may have **one shared-car ride per day and one per week**
(Monday to Sunday), and may not hold a shared car for **more than 24 hours in a row**. Your own
car is never limited.

If a ride is over your share, the form says so and offers **Ask an admin to approve it**. The
ride is then added as **Awaiting approval**: the admins are notified, and you are notified when
they approve or decline it. Until it is approved, a waiting ride does **not** reserve the car,
so someone else can still book that time.

### Before you drive: the Shared car guide

☰ → **Shared car guide** (also **How to charge and drive it** on any shared-car ride) explains,
with pictures:

- **Charging with the company card**: park, hold the card on the reader, plug in, and stop
  with the card before you unplug. Put the card back in the glovebox afterwards.
- **The Mazda 6e for first-timers**: no start button, the gear stalk, regenerative braking,
  the touchscreen controls, the battery gauge, where the charging flap is, and the frunk
  under the bonnet where the cable lives.

### Car status

☰ → **Car status** shows every shared car's **odometer**, **battery**, whether it is **clean**
and any **reported** problems, with who said so and when. Figures marked *about* are guessed
from the rides since the last reading. The same summary appears under the car's name on a
shared-car ride.

Found the car dirty, low on battery or with a warning light? Use **Report on this car** on its
card. A report of a problem goes straight to the admins.

### The odometer (Kilometres)

People in a shared car (and admins) see a **Kilometres** box on the ride:

1. **Note the start:** the driver types the odometer reading when they take the car.
2. **Hand over:** if someone else takes the wheel on the way, enter the reading and pick
   **Who takes the wheel**. The log then shows who drove how far.
3. **Car is back:** the reading when the car is returned, plus **How you leave it**: battery
   %, clean or needs a wash, and **Anything wrong?** This updates *Car status*.

**Undo the last entry** fixes a typo. If you forget, Karpul reminds you a little after the
return time.

### Can't drive after all?

If you remove your own shared-car ride before it leaves and colleagues are booked in it, the
ride is **not** deleted. It keeps the car and shows **Needs a driver**; your passengers are
asked whether one of them drives. The first to tap **I'll drive** becomes the driver. If no
one takes it by departure time, it is removed and everyone is told.

## 7. No car going your way? Ask for a ride

Use **Ask for a ride** (at the top of *Upcoming*, under the filters, and in the **You** panel in *Week*):
the day, roughly when, from and to, and a note ("any time before 9").

Your request shows on the board under that day, among the people **Looking for a ride**. A driver can tap
**Offer a ride**: the ride form opens with your route filled in, and when they save it you
get a seat and a notification. If you find a seat on your own that day, the request is
closed for you. **Withdraw** removes it.

## 8. Notifications, calendar and sharing

### Notifications

☰ → **Settings** → **Notifications**, then **Turn on** (once per browser or phone). Choose what
you want to hear about:

| Category | You are told when |
| --- | --- |
| **Seats** | someone gets in or out of your car, or a seat frees up for you |
| **Ride changes** | a ride you are in moves or is removed, or you are put down to drive |
| **Reminders** | it is time to leave, and to note the odometer when a shared car is back |
| **Messages** | someone writes in the thread of your ride |
| **Ride requests** | a driver offers you a ride you asked for |
| **Shared-car approvals** | (admins) an ask past the share, and the answer to yours |
| **Shared-car reports** | (admins) someone reports a problem with a shared car |

**Before you leave** sets the reminder to 15, 30 or 60 minutes before departure.

On an iPhone, install Karpul to the home screen first (see [section 1](#1-getting-started)).
If the browser says notifications are blocked, allow them in the site settings and try again.

### Calendar

- **Add to calendar** in a ride's ⋮ menu downloads that one ride as a calendar file.
- ☰ → **Your rides** → **Calendar feed** is an address you can subscribe to in Google
  Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar: all your rides, kept up to date by itself. Anyone with
  the address sees your rides, so keep it to yourself. **New address** replaces it if it
  leaks (then subscribe to the new one).

### Sharing a ride

**Share ride** in the ⋮ menu opens the phone's share sheet (or copies the link). The link opens
that ride in Karpul and shows a preview in chat apps. Only signed-in colleagues can see the
ride itself.

### Your rides

☰ → **Your rides** shows rides **Driven**, **Ridden along**, **People carried** and **km shared**,
this month and all time, and your **Past rides**.

## 9. Your account

Tap your name at the top of the ☰ menu to open **Your account**:

- **Photo:** **Add a photo**, then drag and zoom it into the square and **Use this photo**.
  Everyone signed in sees it next to your name.
- **Your name**, **Email**, **Mobile phone**.
- **Password:** changing it signs your other browsers out.
- **Sign out everywhere else**, e.g. after using a shared computer.
- **Sign out.**

**A new version is ready.** When the app has been updated, a card says so. Tap it and trace the
glowing bolt with your finger (or use **Update without the trace**). You can turn this notice
off under **Notifications** → **New version notice**; the app then updates itself the next
time you open it.

**Found a bug, or have an idea?** ☰ → **Help** → **Report a bug or send feedback** writes an
email for you with the details needed to look into it. **Contact support** lists who to call
about a car, the app or organisation.

## 10. For admins

Admins see the **Manage** group in the ☰ menu. An admin can also run any ride: edit it, remove
it and manage its seats, exactly as its driver could. When an admin edits or removes someone's
ride, the driver is notified.

- **Approvals:** shared-car rides waiting for approval, soonest first. A count on the menu
  item (and a dot on ☰) shows when something is waiting. **Approve** reserves the car (refused
  if the car got booked meanwhile); **Decline** removes the ride and tells the driver.
- **Adding a ride for someone else:** in the ride form, tap the driver line and pick a person.
  They become the driver and are notified. Rides an admin adds are never held for approval.
- **Shared cars:** add a car (name, plate, seats), change it, **Retire** a car that left the
  pool (its history stays) or delete one never used. The **Usage** tab shows how each car was
  used over 30, 90 or 365 days and can be downloaded as a spreadsheet.
- **People:** search accounts, **Make admin** / **Remove admin**, create a **Password reset
  link** (pass it on yourself; there is no email), remove someone's photo, or **Delete
  account** (their coming rides are cancelled; past rides keep the name).
- **All rides:** a spreadsheet-style table of every ride in any date range, past included,
  with filters, sorting, the shared cars' odometer and kilometres, and **Download as a
  spreadsheet** (opens correctly in Excel set to German or Serbian).
- **Admin log:** every change an admin made to someone else's ride, a car or an account,
  with the old and new values. Tap a line about a ride to open it.
- **Car reports:** with *Shared-car reports* notifications on, you are told when someone
  reports a problem with a car.

## 11. Questions and troubleshooting

**I can't book the shared car: it says it is taken.** Someone has it at that time. Open the
**Day** view to see when it is free, or pick another car.

**It says I am over my share of the shared car.** Use **Ask an admin to approve it**, or use your
own car. See [The fair share](#the-fair-share).

**I don't get notifications.** Check that they are on in **Notifications** on *this* device,
that the category is on, that the browser has not blocked them, and on an iPhone that Karpul
is installed to the home screen.

**The lamp in the top bar is off.** Live updates are down (often a weak connection). The board
still refreshes itself every 30 seconds until they are back.

**I was signed out.** Either you did not open Karpul for a month, you changed your password
elsewhere, someone used *Sign out everywhere else*, or an admin reset your password. Sign in
again.

**Karpul doesn't open at all when I'm abroad.** Access is limited to the countries the company
works from. Use it from home or the office, or ask support.

**Two people with the same name?** Karpul does not allow two accounts with the same first name
and surname. Register with a distinguishing name (e.g. a middle initial).

Still stuck? ☰ → **Help** → **Contact support**.
